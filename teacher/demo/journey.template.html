How the teacher works

After every call the teacher grades it, then proposes small changes. Code, not the model, decides what is allowed to reach the receptionist.

What it may change

  • Rules in a marked block of the business info
  • Questions it asks the owner
  • Requests for new tools

What it can never do

  • Write prices, hours or policies
  • Enable a tool or set its endpoint
  • Edit its own rubric, weights or limits
  • Change or delete a locked rule

Why changes stay safe

  • Every version is kept; rollback is one pointer move
  • Rejected ideas are logged with the reason
  • Max 3 rule changes per call, 20 rules total
